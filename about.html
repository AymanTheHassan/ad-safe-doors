Our Story

Sydney's sliding door specialists since 1992

You may like to know a bit more about the Adsafe Doors team. We've been successfully repairing sliding doors in Sydney since 1992 — that's over three decades of getting doors back on track.

Adsafe Doors is a privately owned family sliding door repair company. We are dedicated to the highest standards, using the best door technology systems and products, and we are passionate about quality control.

Run by Stephen and Jessie, our small, hands-on team is known across Sydney for friendly, professional service, fair pricing and repairs that genuinely last.
