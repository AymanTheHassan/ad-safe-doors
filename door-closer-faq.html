Door Closer FAQ

Common questions about door closers and how we keep them closing safely and smoothly.

A door closer is the mechanical device that controls how a door shuts — bringing it back to closed in a controlled way and ensuring it latches properly. They're common on entry doors, fire doors and commercial doors.
We service hydraulic surface-mounted closers, overhead transom-mounted closers and floor spring closers, on aluminium, timber and synthetic doors.
