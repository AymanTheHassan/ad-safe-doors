Repair or Replace Your Sliding Door?

Before you spend thousands replacing a door, find out whether a professional repair will get it sliding like new again.

Our Honest Advice

In most cases, repair wins

After being told their door "can't be fixed" and they need to spend thousands on a new one, most of our customers are amazed to learn their existing door can be restored to better-than-new condition.

A quality repair — new heavy-duty rollers, a machined or re-capped track, and proper realignment — typically costs a fraction of full replacement and often outlasts the original.

Get an Honest Assessment

✅ Repair is usually best when…

  • The door frame and glass are sound
  • The door is hard to slide, noisy or has dropped
  • Rollers, tracks or locks have worn out
  • The door is quality-built (even if very old)
  • You want to save thousands of dollars
  • You'd like a warranty on the new rollers

⚠️ Replacement may be needed when…

  • The glass is broken or the seals have failed
  • The frame is corroded or structurally damaged
  • The door is rotted timber beyond repair
  • You're renovating and changing the opening
  • Parts are genuinely unavailable

Even then, we'll give you an honest opinion — we won't recommend a repair that won't last.

Why repairing makes sense

Many doors we attend are 25, 30 or even 50 years old. The frames on these older doors are often heavy, high-quality aluminium that simply doesn't get made the same way today. The only thing that's failed is the running gear — the rollers and track — which are wear items.

By upgrading to 4-wheel rollers (which spread the load of the door across more wheels) and machining or capping the track with stainless steel, we give an old door a new lease on life. Many customers tell us their repaired door runs better than it ever did when new — and glides with a single finger.

Warranty: We back our roller work with up to a 5-year warranty, so you can have peace of mind long after we've left.
