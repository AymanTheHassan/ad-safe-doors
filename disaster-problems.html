Sliding Door Disaster Problems

Recognise your door? These are the most common sliding door disasters we rescue across Sydney — and almost all of them can be repaired.

🛑

1. Impossible to Open

Your door needs two hands and a heave to move. Usually collapsed rollers or a damaged track.

🔊

2. Grinding & Noisy

That 'freight train' sound means the rollers are worn and dragging on the track.

⬇️

3. Door Has Dropped

The door scrapes the floor or sits crooked because the rollers have failed.

🚪

4. Off the Track

The door has jumped or fallen out of its track and won't sit back in place.

🔩

5. Broken Rollers

Wheels are cracked, seized or completely destroyed — common in coastal/sandy areas.

〰️

6. Damaged Track

The aluminium track is worn, dented or mushroomed and chews up new rollers.

🔒

7. Won't Lock

The door no longer aligns with the frame so the latch and bolt won't engage.

🪟

8. Flyscreen Issues

Screen doors jamming, falling out or with broken rollers and corner stays.

↔️

9. Out of Alignment

Door is lopsided and rubs the frame, making opening and locking a struggle.

🏋️

10. Heavy Stacker Doors

Large, heavy glass stacker doors that have become a chore to slide.

🌊

11. Sand & Grit Damage

Backyard sand and debris have destroyed the runners and track.

🕰️

12. Old & Worn Out

25–50 year old doors that have never been serviced and are at the end of the line.

Good news: No matter how bad it looks, most of these problems are repairable — saving you thousands compared to a full door replacement.
