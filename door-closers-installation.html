Get the right closer for your door

Choosing and fitting a door closer isn't one-size-fits-all. The correct closer depends on the size and weight of the door, how it's used, and whether it needs to meet safety or compliance requirements.

We supply and install quality hydraulic surface-mounted, overhead transom-mounted and floor spring closers, then adjust them precisely so your door closes smoothly, quietly and latches every time.

Why have it installed by a specialist?

  • Correct selection — the right closer size and strength for your door.
  • Proper mounting — fitted securely so it lasts and doesn't tear out.
  • Precise adjustment — closing and latch speed set for safety and comfort.
  • Compliance — important for fire and accessibility doors in commercial settings.
