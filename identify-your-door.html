Quick Checklist

What to look for

1

Check the material

Is the frame aluminium, timber, or a mix of timber and glass? This tells us a lot about the running gear.

2

Find the markings

Look on the edge of the door, the lock, or the inside of the track for a brand name or stamp.

3

Note the style

Standard sliding, stacker, cavity, or patio door? Count how many panels slide.

4

Measure roughly

Approximate height and width, and whether it's a single, double or extra-heavy door.

Common door types we see

  • Aluminium & glass sliding doors — the most common door across Sydney homes and units.
  • Stacker doors — multiple large panels that stack behind one another.
  • Patio sliding doors — often leading to courtyards, balconies and decks.
  • Timber & glass sliding doors — classic timber-framed doors, often in older homes.
  • Flyscreen / security sliding doors — lighter screens that run alongside the main door.
Tip: Take a quick photo of your door and the track on your phone. When you call us, you can describe what you see and we'll know exactly what to bring.
