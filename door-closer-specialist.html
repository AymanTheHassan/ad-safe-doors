Closer Experts

Expert door closer repairs

A door closer is the device that controls how your door shuts — keeping it safe, quiet and properly latched. When it fails, doors slam, won't latch, or stay open when they shouldn't.

As door closer specialists, we diagnose the fault and repair or adjust your closer so it works smoothly and safely once again. No matter if you have an aluminium, timber or synthetic door, our commercial and domestic service is available across Sydney.

Types We Service

All major door closer types

🔧

Hydraulic Surface-Mounted

The most common closers, fitted to the face of the door or frame. We repair leaks, sticking arms and adjust the closing speed.

🚪

Overhead Transom-Mounted

Concealed closers mounted in the transom above the door. We service and adjust these for smooth, controlled closing.

⬇️

Floor Spring Closers

Heavy-duty closers set into the floor, common on glass and entry doors. We repair and re-tension these for reliable operation.

Common door closer problems we fix

  • Doors that slam shut too hard or too fast
  • Doors that no longer latch or close fully
  • Closers leaking hydraulic oil
  • Stiff, sticking or noisy closing action
  • Closing speed needs adjusting for safety or comfort
  • Worn or broken closer arms and brackets
Safety note: A faulty closer on a fire or security door can be a safety and compliance risk. We'll make sure yours closes correctly every time.
